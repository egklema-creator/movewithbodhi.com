About Bodhi

One Platform. Every Practice.

At Move with Bodhi, we believe movement is medicine. We've built a space where beginners feel welcome, experienced practitioners are challenged, and busy people finally find their balance.

I'm Bodhi — a yoga teacher drawing from Hatha, Tai Chi, and Kundalini traditions, woven together with a deep reverence for the spiritual and a commitment to making practice accessible to every body, every day.

From energizing yoga flows and full-body fitness workouts to guided meditation and mindfulness sessions — everything you need to feel better in your body and clearer in your mind is right here. Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or deepening a lifelong practice, you belong here.
